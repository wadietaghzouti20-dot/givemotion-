## Render and quality-control instructions
1. Open the [Givemotion Actions workflow](https://github.com/wadietaghzouti20-dot/givemotion-/actions/workflows/render.yml).
2. Choose **Render Givemotion video** / **RENDEVO Daily Reels**, then select **Run workflow** if no run has started automatically.
3. The workflow installs Givemotion, Chrome and FFmpeg; generates the French and English neural voiceovers; creates original background audio; renders HTML/CSS motion graphics; burns in synchronized subtitles; and encodes H.264 MP4.
4. Expected files: `RENDEVO_FR_2026-11-15.mp4` and `RENDEVO_EN_2026-11-15.mp4`.
5. Verify each file is 1080×1920, H.264, 30 fps and 15–30 seconds. Check voice/subtitle sync and review the sample UI before publishing.
6. Download the `RENDEVO-Daily-Reels-2026-11-15` artifact from the completed Actions run. Never publish before a human review.

The current verified RENDEVO workflow includes online appointment requests, availability/conflict checks, an admin tracking sheet and configurable email confirmations. Do not claim live deployments in every sector, customer results, testimonials, payments or integrations that have not been verified.
